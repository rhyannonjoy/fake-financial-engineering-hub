## Pre-MIP

Discuss deployment scheduling to ensure a smooth MIP. Discuss DFR
coverage, especially if MIP falls on a weekend and or federal holiday.
Ensure that everyone has access to the team's internal
databases:

- **QA**: `invoicingdbtest`
- **PROD**: `invoicingdb`

For any data changes, visit “How to Maintain invoicingdb”
